I am a PhD student in Chemistry (Scientific Computation) at the Department of Chemistry, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I joined in August 2026. I work with Prof. Lixue Cheng on AI for energy materials. Previously, I completed my master's studies in Materials Physics and Chemistry at Peking University (2023–2026) and my B.Eng. in Materials Science and Engineering at Wuhan University of Technology (2019–2023).

My research combines first-principles calculations and data-driven AI to understand energy materials and guide materials discovery. My current and previous work includes:

- **Multi-Agent Electrolyte Design**: An LLM-based platform that integrates literature retrieval, expert-agent reasoning, and iterative optimization of lithium battery electrolyte formulations, including insights from ionic-liquid coordination chemistry and ion transport.
- **AI for Solid-State Electrolytes**: Large-scale literature classification, extraction and standardization of material properties, and ongoing development of a generative design framework.
- **Computational Electrochemistry**: DFT and ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) studies of C–C coupling in CO₂ electroreduction and confined proton transport in layered manganese oxide electrodes.

I use VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO, and Gaussian for first-principles calculations, alongside Python, machine learning, natural language processing, and retrieval-augmented generation for materials research. Further details are available in my [CV](/cv/) and [publications](/publications/).
